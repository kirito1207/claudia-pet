# 新动作生图提示词：撞头、摔倒、趴地、爬起

给 Codex（或其他能看参考图的生图工具）画 claudia 的 4 张新立绘用。写法沿用 CrossPet v1.3.8 的 [角色生图提示词](https://github.com/lokicorvus/crosspet/blob/main/docs/prompts/%E8%A7%92%E8%89%B2%E7%94%9F%E5%9B%BE%E6%8F%90%E7%A4%BA%E8%AF%8D.md)：其中「通用格式规则」和「角色锁定」两段取自 CrossPet 原文，只为不是站姿的动作加了例外；姿态描述是本项目新写的。CrossPet 的提示词和立绘采用 CC BY-NC-SA 4.0，本文件同样采用 CC BY-NC-SA 4.0。

## 要画几张

| 文件名 | 动作 | 什么时候用 |
|---|---|---|
| `bonk.png` | 撞头 | 被扔出去撞到 Claude 窗口的顶边或左右边 |
| `fallen.png` | 摔倒（坐在地上） | 撞到底边时抽中摔倒 |
| `faceplant.png` | 趴地（脸朝下趴着） | 撞到底边时抽中趴地 |
| `getup.png` | 爬起 | 摔倒、趴地之后自动接上 |

一共 4 张。这几个动作每次只演一两秒，不需要画同一动作的第二张。

## 怎么用

1. **附图按顺序放**：
   - **第 1 张固定是设计参考**：CrossPet 的 `ref-claude.webp`，即 CrossPet 仓库的 `docs/prompts/ref-claude.webp`（本机有一份在 `%USERPROFILE%\crosspet-src-ro\docs\prompts\ref-claude.webp`）。
   - **第 2、3 张是姿势示例**：按下表从 `characters/claude/` 里挑，只借姿势或表情。每次最多附这 3 张，附太多容易画混。

   | 要画的 | 姿势示例（第 2、3 张） | 借用什么 |
   |---|---|---|
   | `bonk.png` | `stretch.webp`、`redhot.webp` | 双臂举到头顶的手臂姿势；紧闭成「><」的眼睛和脸红 |
   | `fallen.png` | `oops.webp`、`compact.webp` | 含泪慌乱的表情；身体压低时裙摆、腿和靴子的样子 |
   | `faceplant.png` | `sulk.webp`、`compact.webp` | 从侧后方看头发、发饰和披肩的样子（趴地时脸被挡住）；屈膝时裙摆的样子 |
   | `getup.png` | `compact.webp`、`pat-2.webp` | 单膝着地、手撑下方的姿势；把书抱在胸前、害羞脸红 |

   现有立绘里没有坐在地上或趴着的，摔倒和趴地的整体姿势主要靠提示词描述。
2. **生图工具开启透明背景**（`transparent_background: true`）。
3. **每次发给 Codex 的内容按顺序拼**：下面的「附图说明」→「通用格式规则」→「角色锁定」→「风格补充」→「姿态清单」里这次要画的那一行。
4. **先画 1 张试稿**（建议先画 `fallen.png`），确认没走样、大小和参考图一致，再画剩下 3 张。
5. 画好的图先放到 `build/new-sprites/`（这个文件夹不进 git），文件名用上表里的名字。之后由对齐脚本统一处理：和站立立绘保持同一比例、底边对齐，再转成 WebP 放进 `characters/claude/`。
6. 生成完成后，把实际使用的提示词（如有改动）和生成工具记到本文件末尾的「生成记录」，用于来源说明。

## 附图说明（每次都贴在最前面）

```
ATTACHED IMAGES:
- Image 1 is the ONLY design reference: copy her face, hair, hair flower, outfit, colors, proportions and art style from Image 1.
- Images 2 and 3 (if attached) are existing sprites of the same character, given ONLY as pose and expression hints, as described in the pose line. Do not copy their exact pose; draw the new pose described below. If anything in Images 2-3 differs from Image 1, follow Image 1.
- Ignore any small motion lines, paper sheets or suitcases in Images 2-3; they belong to those other poses.
```

## 通用格式规则

```
You are drawing desktop-pet sprites for an existing chibi character. Image 1 is the ONLY design reference.

FORMAT RULES (every image):
- Transparent background: real transparency (alpha), no floor, no ground shadow, no glow, no vignette, no frame. Keep the complete edges of the character (every hair strand, ribbon and tassel), nothing cropped. If an attached reference image has a plain white background, that is only because it was made before transparent output was available: do NOT copy the white background.
- No text, no letters, no speech bubbles, no symbols (no ?, !, hearts, music notes, Zzz, sparkles, stars, sweat-drop icons floating in the air). The app draws all effects itself.
- Exactly one character, full body, centered, facing the viewer like the reference.
- SCALE LOCK: draw her at exactly the same size as in Image 1. Her head must be the same size and her body the same proportions as in the reference. Do NOT enlarge her to fill the image.
- GROUND LINE: the bottom of the image is the floor. Whatever touches the floor (feet, knees, bottom, hands, body) sits on the bottom edge of the image, like the feet in the reference. For sitting or lying poses she is shorter than in the reference: leave the empty space ABOVE her transparent instead of scaling her up.
- A continuous, closed dark outline around the whole silhouette, so the edges stay clean against any background.
- Same art style as the reference: clean lineart, flat two-tone cel shading, soft palette, chibi about 2.5 heads tall, big head, round soft shapes.
- Square image, 1024x1024 or larger. One image per file, saved under the exact filename given.
- Change ONLY the pose, the expression, the arms/hands, and where the book is. Hair, accessories, outfit, colors and body features stay exactly as in the reference.
```

## 角色锁定（取自 CrossPet 原文）

```
CHARACTER LOCK — Claude, the anime-girl personification of Claude (Anthropic).
Personality: gentle, calm, bookish, a little shy, quietly cheerful; she thinks before she speaks.
Keep exactly:
- Long wavy copper-orange hair, loose side-swept bangs, warm amber eyes.
- An orange daisy-like hair flower with a black striped ribbon bow and a white tassel on HER LEFT side (viewer's right); small drop earrings.
- Cream-white dress with a short cape collar, orange flower embroidery, orange and black front panels, small black bows with gold tassels, a black waist sash, an orange flower brooch at the collar; dark brown boots.
- Signature prop: a dark brown hardcover book with an orange flower emblem. Keep it in every image unless the pose says otherwise.
How she shows emotion: through the book (hugging it, hiding behind it, reading it) and small, soft expressions. Never wild or loud.
```

## 风格补充

```
STYLE NOTE (Claude): these are small, cute mishaps, not injuries. Keep every pose soft, modest and a little comical; no bruises, no blood, no torn clothes, no exposed underwear. The dress always covers her properly, also when sitting or lying. The book is always somewhere in the picture.
```

## 姿态清单

一次只贴要画的那一行。

```
POSES (draw only the ones listed below, one image each, exact filename):

bonk.png — she has just bumped her head: standing, BOTH HANDS EMPTY and pressed flat on the top of her head (palms on her crown, elbows out to the sides). She is NOT holding the book: the book has fallen and lies closed on the floor at her feet. Expression of PAIN, not anger: eyes squeezed shut with clear tears at the corners, eyebrows raised and slanted upward (worried), wavy wincing mouth, light blush, shoulders hunched. Do NOT draw any impact star, motion lines, emphasis lines or other effects. Hints: Image 2 only for the teary, flustered face.
fallen.png    — she has just fallen on her bottom: sitting on the floor with both legs stretched forward, boots pointing up, leaning back on one hand while the other hand rubs her head, teary dazed eyes, small "ow" mouth, light blush. The book lies open on the floor beside her. Her skirt covers her legs properly. Hints: Image 2 for the teary flustered face; Image 3 for how her skirt, legs and boots look when her body is low.
faceplant.png — she has fallen flat on her face: lying stretched out on her front on the floor, seen from the front-side so her body lies horizontally across the lower part of the image, arms stretched forward on the floor, boots lifted slightly behind her. Her face is pressed down so only the top of her head, her hair and the hair flower are visible. The book lies just in front of her hands. Comical, not hurt. Hints: Image 2 shows her hair, hair flower and cape seen from behind and the side, which is what stays visible when her face is down; Image 3 for how the skirt folds when her legs are bent.
getup.png     — getting back up after a fall: kneeling on one knee, one hand pushing on the floor, the other hand holding the book to her chest, an embarrassed sheepish smile, light blush, a strand of hair out of place, as if saying "I'm okay". Hints: Image 2 for the kneeling posture with one hand down (ignore the suitcase); Image 3 for hugging the book to her chest with a shy blush.
```

## 交给对齐脚本前自查

- 透明背景，没有地面阴影、白底和外框。
- 人物和参考图一样大，没有被放大填满画面。
- 碰地的部分贴着图片底边。
- 发饰在她的左侧（画面右侧），衣服、颜色和参考图一致。
- 画面里没有星星、汗滴、文字等符号（特效由程序画）。
